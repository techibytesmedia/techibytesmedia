# 📜 Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the **Techibytes Media website**, we pledge to foster a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment.

## ✅ Our Standards

- Use inclusive, professional, and respectful language
- Be patient and open-minded during collaboration
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus discussions on the project and the needs of its users
- Respect differing experiences, perspectives, and backgrounds

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or violence
- Personal insults or targeted intimidation
- Racist, sexist, discriminatory, or demeaning remarks
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, commits, reviews, or discussions

## 📣 Reporting Issues

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the Techibytes Media team:

📧 **support@techibytesmedia.com**

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

## 👥 Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code. Violations may result in a warning, temporary restriction, removal from the project, or a permanent ban from future participation.
